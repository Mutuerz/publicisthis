Is this AI? Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The short version: Is this AI? checks your pictures and text on your phone. Nothing you check is uploaded, and we don't collect any information about you.

What the app does with what you check

When you pick a photo, take a photo, open a document or paste text, the app checks it right on your device. Your pictures, documents and text are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The phone apps don't send anything over the internet.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share personal information, and we don't use your content to train anything.

What stays on your phone

  • Past cases: for up to your last 50 checks, the app keeps a small preview (a tiny thumbnail or the first lines of the text) and the verdict, so you can look back at them.
  • Settings the app needs to work.

This stays in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete past cases at any time with Clear on the Past cases screen, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions

  • Photos: the app only sees the photos and files you choose to check.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo to check.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a case, the app makes a case card and hands it to your phone's share menu. It goes only where you send it.

Web version

The test version at mutuerz.github.io runs in your browser and also checks everything on your device. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

A note on results

Verdicts are a best guess, not proof. No AI checker is always right, so please don't use a verdict alone to accuse someone or to make an important decision.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a future version adds a feature that sends anything off your phone, the app will tell you first and this page will say exactly what is sent.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the support page.
