Common questions

Is the verdict always right?
No. Every verdict is a best guess based on the clues Sherlock finds. Treat it as a clue, not proof.

Why did I get "Can't tell"?
The clues disagreed, or there wasn't enough to go on. Very short text and pictures that were heavily edited or sent through chat apps are the hardest.

What do "Leans AI" and "Leans human" mean?
The clues mostly point one way, but not strongly enough for a firmer answer.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Every check runs on your phone. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my past cases?
Open Past cases and tap Clear, or uninstall the app.
